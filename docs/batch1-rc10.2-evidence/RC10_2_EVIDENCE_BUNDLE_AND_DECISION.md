## 3. Summary of P0 Engineering Changes in RC10.2

1. **P0.1 | Generalized Legal Issue & Temporal Routing** (`src/core/knowledge/case-fact-ledger.ts`, `src/core/knowledge/legal-issue-fact-analyzer.ts`, `src/core/knowledge/query.ts`, `src/core/knowledge/retrieval.ts`):
   - **Request `applicable_date` Parity & Conflict Detection**: `buildStructuredCaseFactLedger()` now treats request-supplied `applicable_date` identically to a prompt-extracted date when no conflicting prompt date exists (unless the prompt states the date is unknown), and flags cross-window prompt-vs-request VAT date conflicts with `is_ambiguous = true` and `CLARIFICATION_OR_CONDITIONAL_NEEDED` (supplying both decrees as `conditional_branch_authority` rather than silently choosing one).
   - **Retrieval Temporal Alignment**: `query.ts` now passes the fact-ledger's resolved `controlling_tax_point.iso_date` (or `null` on date conflicts) into `retrieveAuthorizedKnowledge()`, eliminating stale-decree leaks on prompt-date-only and multi-turn fact-change queries (`F-08`).
   - **Clean VAT vs WHT & Regime Separation**: Separated domestic standard-rate VAT from export/zero-rating (`§80/1` + `DG VAT 105`), inbound cross-border services (`ภ.พ.36 §77/2 + §83/6`), exemptions (`§81`), mixed-use (`§82/6`), explicit statutory lookups (`มาตรา 80/1 (1)/(2)`), and primary WHT queries containing incidental VAT wording (`"ก่อนภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม"`, `"ยังไม่รวม VAT"`, `"จดทะเบียน VAT"`). Fixed Thai grammar bug where `ลูกค้าในประเทศ` prefix-matched foreign country names like `ลูกค้าในประเทศเกาหลีใต้`. Added government-payer WHT routing (`§69 ทวิ` + `§50`).
   - **Hierarchy of Authority & Fact-Driven Property Transfers**: Primary Revenue Code provisions in `controlling_work_ids` now outrank secondary `TaxRuling` authorities in `retrieval.ts`. `prov.th.rc.54` is derived strictly from asset + relationship + withholding/shortfall relevance (`recipientRole !== "LEGITIMATE_CHILD"` OR value `> 20,000,000` THB OR explicit WHT/shortfall question — zero blanket `§54` on exempt `≤ 20M` father→legitimate-child transfers like `F-07`).
2. **P0.2 | Hardened Grader v2 & Ranking-vs-Packet-vs-Answer Separation** (`src/core/knowledge/retrieval-benchmark-grader.ts`):
   - Explicitly reports `ranking_passed`, `actual_packet_passed`, `ranking_miss_packet_recovered` (`first_failing_step: "RANKING_MISS_PACKET_RECOVERED"`), and `final_answer_qa_status: "NOT_RUN_PRE_LLM_CAPTURE"`, covered by a new unit test in `tests/knowledge/retrieval-benchmark-grader-negative-controls.test.ts`.
3. **P0.3 | Hosted Smoke Runner Fix, DB-Backed Reopen Guard & Real Live Neon Transactional Proof** (`src/core/knowledge/hosted-smoke-verifier.ts`, `scripts/run-hosted-rc10-smoke.ts`, `src/app/api/v1/knowledge/answers/[id]/route.ts`, `src/core/knowledge/claim-traceability-engine.ts`, `scripts/verify-neon-transactional-save-live.ts`):
   - Fixed reopen endpoint to `GET /api/v1/knowledge/answers/:id?require_db=1` (rejecting local-memory fallback when `require_db=1` is requested and returning `persistence_source: "neon_postgres"` + `x-reg24-persistence-source` header).
   - Fixed payload field parsing (`answer.trace_id`, `answer.status`, top-level `execution_provenance`, `traceable_answer.answer_revision`), enforced start+end build-pin verification, and added 4 fail-closed Vitest contract tests (`tests/knowledge/hosted-smoke-runner-contract.test.ts`).
   - Proved interrupted-save atomic rollback, clean commit + fresh DB reopen, idempotent retry, and append-only immutability trigger against **real live staging Neon PostgreSQL** (zero mocks!).
